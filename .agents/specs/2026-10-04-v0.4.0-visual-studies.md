# Sheg v0.4.0 visual studies

Status: approved architectural specification, approved on 2026-10-12. This consolidates the visual-study discussion and supersedes the [exploratory sketch](2026-10-04-v0.4.0-visual-studies-sketch.md) as the planning input. It does not certify provider feasibility, authorize paid inference, or begin implementation. The stable v0.3.0 release is the compatibility baseline. This specification supplies the design basis for an executable roadmap; provider feasibility gates remain part of that work.

## 1. Intent and release boundary

Sheg helps an author investigate whether a visual communicates the intended meaning and supports the intended audience's next decision. An article image may earn interest, clarify an argument, or interrupt reading. A rendered page may make a next section easier to identify. A character sketch may express the intended traits or fit the character's actions in a script. These are applications of one domain-neutral foundation: frozen text and visual material, authored typed questions, stable respondent profiles, and inspectable comparisons.

The initial author story is: "My article has five sections and an image between sections 3 and 4. Does it help respondents continue, distract them, or complement the article?" Success means the author can investigate keeping, removing, moving, replacing, captioning, or presenting that image while seeing the evidence, respondent differences, and limits of the comparison. The author owns the decision and the criteria for what the image should accomplish.

The v0.4.0 release includes static image inputs, a scaling-only preparation tool, a Clef decision adapter, capability-driven provider selection within studies, multimodal packet admission and inspection, durable visual custody, matched branching, compact visual carry-forward, visual follow-ons, comparison evidence, and shipped agent guidance. Article imagery and placement are the first acceptance scenario. Rendered A/B and character brief/script fit are required demonstrations of the same primitives, subject to the perceptual and context feasibility gates below. They do not introduce separate runtimes or hard-coded editorial questions.

The released baseline is v0.3.0 at tag `5a3768c989ba4f6d5363fa69471a462c82beec6b`, published on 2026-10-05 and reconciled into `develop`. The design reconciliation uses `origin/develop` at `c170969`, inspected on 2026-10-12, with package identity `0.3.1-dev.1`. Released persistence uses Drizzle-backed typed repositories and SQLite schema 9 under ADR-0030 and ADR-0031. Durable journeys admit initial packets and check later turns when reached under ADR-0032; historic exhaustive manifest preflight remains a separate diagnostic. Preserve existing text-only studies, typed answer semantics, independent question groups, graph exposure rules, durable follow-ons, attempt accounting, recovery, and self-contained plugin distribution.

### Non-goals

- Video, animation, browser navigation, automatic screenshot capture, design editing, or image generation.
- A gaze predictor, human reading-time simulator, population estimator, conversion optimizer, or proof of human learning.
- A persistent model respondent, simulated forgetting, or invisible image-memory service.
- Undeclared provider substitution, automatic fallback after dispatch, self-hosted Clef deployment, or fine-tuning.
- Free-form reasoning generated by Clef, automatic trait extraction, or an objective character-design winner.
- Comparing five or more character designs through multiple calls or a contact-sheet workaround. Start with up to four designs; investigate larger comparisons in a later iteration.
- A new GUI, spend-accounting subsystem, general image-editing toolkit, automatic alt-text generation, or changes to the released v0.3.0 product.

## 2. Approach and alternatives

Extend material and packet contracts to carry visuals and make mixed-provider execution and matched branching first-class within one durable study. Provider choice is an execution input, independent of the domain meaning of a stage. An agent authors the experiment, its changing variables, its comparison membership, exposure boundaries, and overrides. Sheg selects eligible configured providers, packs calls, and preserves execution evidence under those constraints. One study can use Jev for text, Clef for a pixel-dependent comparison, and an eligible text provider afterward without requiring another study merely to change capability.

Agent-composed follow-ons remain useful for experiments designed after a run, but are not the only way to cross providers. A run-wide provider lock remains an explicit supported choice, including existing requests. Treating every visual only as text remains unsuitable for layout, typeface, sketch appearance, or image/text contrast. Descriptions serve compact carry-forward and explicit described-evidence questions, not substitutes for pixel-dependent evaluation.

### Provider selection and experiment constraints

Automatic selection considers only explicitly enabled configurations from a secret-free local catalogue. Each configuration declares input capabilities, typed question/group support, route/model identity, effective admission bounds and measurement availability, readiness, local or hosted execution, and supported price/operational metadata with an evidence basis. Future adapters participate through this contract rather than visual-workflow special cases. Local inference has no provider invoice, not zero resource cost. No model-quality or latency score is inferred from a name or parameter count.

The agent-facing contract explains defaults and controls without requiring routing internals. With no stronger preference, Sheg chooses economically among available, capability-compatible, admitting providers. An agent may pin a configuration for the entire study or a selected evaluation/group, express a preferred order, require capabilities, or deliberately compare providers. Apply hard experimental/capability/fit constraints first, then explicit selection, per-request preferences, saved user preferences, and economy. A binding selection never silently falls back. Credentials are not part of visible selection evidence.

Declared stimulus or respondent comparisons use the same provider configuration and model across their members by default. The agent teaches Sheg which evaluations belong together; Sheg does not infer a comparison from wording or nearby graph nodes. Changing the stimulus or respondent holds the provider/model fixed along with other declared constants. An explicit provider-comparison design may relax that equality and must report model variation as part of the treatment. For an image/no-image comparison, capability eligibility covers both arms together, so both use the same vision-capable provider. This default applies to separate-variant and direct-comparison studies without making their distinct evidence interchangeable.

Outside declared equality constraints, provider selection may differ between evaluation units, including reached journey turns. A unit is a respondent's independently scored question or same-state question group. A comparison constraint may bind several such units across respondents and branches. A stage boundary alone neither requires nor permits a model change inside a locked comparison. User/agent choices and same-provider locks take precedence over economical routing, but cannot bypass capability or admission.

Freeze the enabled configuration catalogue, connection/model settings, preference order, experimental constraints, supported metadata/estimate basis, and selection-policy version at study acceptance. Setup does not implicitly enable providers or models. Missing catalogue/no eligible entries returns setup guidance for automatic requests; existing explicit provider-config requests remain supported without a catalogue migration. An explicit disable policy cannot be bypassed by embedding the disabled configuration. Later catalogue changes do not alter an accepted study. Secrets remain outside these snapshots; credential rotation may serve the same frozen destination/configuration.

Resolve automatic choices against actual provider-visible inputs as units are reached. Persist the resolved configuration, relevant fit and exclusion evidence, and policy/snapshot identity before dispatch. Subsequent retries and recovery preserve that choice rather than reselecting after an error. For a comparison, resolve one common provider/model before its first dispatch and preserve it for all members. If actual inputs for its bounded participating cohort are available, check the common choice against all those inputs. A comparison spanning future sequential turns still uses reached-turn admission; it does not promise fit for unseen histories. If a later member does not fit the locked provider, report a zero-call failure rather than switching models or excluding the member from the declared design.

Readiness is checked at selection and dispatch: a frozen catalogue does not promise that a service or credential stays available. No eligible candidate produces actionable non-inference failure evidence. A resolved provider becoming unavailable does not authorize substitution. Resume can retry that configuration under existing recovery controls; an intentionally changed provider or experiment requires an explicit new execution/continuation with disclosed lineage, not rewriting accepted evidence.

Select within the remaining study-wide physical-call allowance, considering the planned batching control and all ready members governed by one choice. Prefer eligible local execution without an invoice; among hosted candidates use comparable known estimated charges for those actual packets, including repeated state across splits. Unknown/incomparable prices are disclosed and use a documented deterministic order, without claiming cheapest execution. Inspection distinguishes estimates for resolved inputs from topology-based future call bounds and unresolved future routing. No exhaustive response-history enumeration or invented complete-study price is required. Provider choices cannot solve budget exhaustion by changing the experimental condition.

Before execution, inspection explains configured defaults, comparison locks, overrides, resolved initial choices, rejected alternatives, and unresolved future selections. A fingerprint pins the inspected inputs, frozen catalogue/policy, and constraints; it does not pretend to pin choices dependent on unknown future answers. Acceptance refuses a stale pinned fingerprint. Idempotent submissions return the original accepted snapshot and any already resolved choices. Evidence always distinguishes planned selection from the provider/model that actually served each evaluation.

### Question grouping and physical batching

Preserve ADR-0021's division: the agent authors independent questions over the same frozen state, while Sheg plans and executes fitting physical batches for the selected provider. Poll and follow-on groups may split their ordered questions across calls without changing their material or feeding sibling answers into history. Laya's current singleton behavior and Jev's measured batching participate in selection estimates. Journey turns remain sequential within each branch; the same independent-group contract can be authored at a journey stage without making its questions sequential. Sheg must not infer independence from similar question wording or combine different respondent contexts.

Add an explicit execution control for automatic batching versus singleton calls within an independent group. Automatic batching is the default and keeps the current fitting-split behavior; singleton forbids joint scoring while preserving logical shared-context/evaluation identities. Provider selection and call-fit inspection honor that control and the remaining study-wide maximum physical-call allowance, including matched branches and retries. Inspection reports the planned minimum calls, included question IDs per planned call, and repeated-input estimate. Actual dispatches remain recorded physical-attempt evidence. Resume may regroup remaining unanswered questions under the accepted batching control and the same provider, as current execution does; it cannot change singleton into joint execution or replay successful sibling answers.

The agent controls semantic dependencies, visibility, grouping, and whether joint evaluation is allowed. Sheg controls wire packing under those constraints. Clef joint-scoring effects belong in feasibility checks and guidance; no claim of statistical independence follows from no sibling-answer history. If a recipe needs singleton evaluation, use the explicit control, not hidden execution changes.

The proposed first Clef transport is Cloudflare Workers AI REST from the existing Node runtime. Use an explicit Clef provider identity, Cloudflare account configuration, and a separately stored credential. Support the documented `clef` and `clef-flash` model selectors without silently swapping them; use `clef` as the initial spike reference model. An enabled `clef-flash` entry may participate in economy-based selection when its capability/admission contract is established. Choosing a selector never enables it implicitly. Clef through OpenRouter and local Clef hosting are outside this release's required routing scope; existing Jev routes remain supported. Successful compact-image REST probes establish basic transport feasibility, not a general admitted-input envelope. The hosted feasibility gate must establish that envelope before retained integration depends on it. A route change requires a design amendment rather than an invisible fallback.

## 3. User-value slices and study semantics

### Article imagery and hypothetical placement

An author supplies ordered article sections, the actual image, its caption if present, and clearly labelled insertion points. A shared-state study exposes the whole article and image. Independently authored questions ask how the same respondent would judge placement A, B, and so on. For example: "Assume this image appears only at point A. Would this respondent want to continue from section 1 into section 2?" Exact point definitions and conditional instructions are frozen material/question content. No new placement engine is required.

Separate relevance, continuity, distraction, comprehension, and expressed interest. Include removal or no-fit alternatives when the author's decision warrants them. A Choice can select a placement; Scores can assess authored dimensions; Noul can assess an explicitly defined proposition. No universal attractiveness score or automatic ranking formula is prescribed.

The whole-article study is an informed hypothetical assessment. It cannot be reported as a first encounter with unseen later sections. A staged journey instead exposes only material reached on that path. Independent questions do not append sibling answers to history. Clef's joint scoring still needs validation for sibling-question influence; conceptual independence is not proof of statistical independence.

### Rendered visual A/B

An author supplies frozen captures of two visual treatments, such as different font sizes or image-left versus image-right. Separate-variant evaluation sends A and B in separate contexts with equivalent respondent profiles, prior state, question, rubric, provider, and model. Neither sees the other variant or its answer. A declared matched fork within one study copies each participating pre-treatment context into both conditions. Separately composed runs remain supported with common source lineage and exact criteria; the release does not require a new automatic matcher for unrelated historic runs.

A direct comparison shows both variants together and asks an authored preference question. It is a different task. Neutral labels, image order, and any contact-sheet arrangement are recorded inputs. Tie or neither options are available through authored Choice options. Do not equate direct preference with independently judged adequacy.

Capture provenance includes supplied viewport dimensions, capture bounds, render scale, and treatment notes where known. Record unavailable provenance explicitly rather than fabricating it. Font-loading readiness and relevant font identity belong in the author/agent's capture notes. Wrapping, height, or viewport-content changes caused by font size are part of the treatment. Whole redesigns are allowed, but their results concern the bundle of changes. Text supplied beside a screenshot can bypass the screenshot's readability; the agent must select and disclose the appropriate evidence for the question.

### Character brief and script fit

An author supplies a character brief such as "heroic and intelligent" and up to four sketches. Separate authored dimensions reveal whether a sketch communicates one trait while missing another. Additional typed options can investigate unintended impressions. Unprompted trait reading occurs in a context without the target traits; prompted brief-fit occurs in a separate context with them. Neither result substitutes for the other.

A script can supply narrative evidence: behavior, dialogue, relationships, role, and tone. Identify the character being matched when the script contains several. Distinguish matching a specified character from asking which design communicates protagonist prominence. A design can look like a lead while poorly fitting the written character. Ask diagnostic dimensions alongside overall fit where useful; do not promise free-form explanations from the decision model.

Assess up to four sketches separately using the same narrative and respondent basis, or show all four together for an explicitly authored direct comparison. Current hosted Clef permits four images per request; any additional visual context also consumes that allowance. The v0.4.0 recipe stays within this envelope rather than attempting five candidates or using a contact sheet to bypass the scope. Comparing five or more designs across multiple calls is a later iteration that must establish how the comparison remains meaningful across differing visible alternatives. Sheg must not automatically split a direct comparison into unrelated calls. Whole-script feasibility depends on admitted context; any selected excerpt is an explicit change of evidence, not hidden truncation.

Pairwise comparison is a promising authoring primitive within that initial four-design scope. Four designs yield six unordered pairs. Each pair receives the same respondent profile, brief/script evidence, criteria, and pre-comparison history, with only its two candidate sketches exposed. It asks a relative preference question, including ties or neither where appropriate. Pair calls branch independently; earlier pair answers and absent candidates do not become evidence in later pairs. An all-four call instead exposes all four alternatives and asks one selection question. These are distinct experimental conditions, not interchangeable execution plans for the same frozen input. Sheg must not substitute one for the other through automatic batching or economical provider selection.

Spike all-four selection against the complete pairwise set during implementation before deciding whether both comparison primitives belong in the release. The user expects they may differ enough to justify both, but no outcome is established. Keep provider/model and narrative/respondent basis fixed; retain direct choices, pair outcomes, coverage, and presentation-order effects. Pairwise results can contain ties, cycles, or no consistent winner. No tournament, ranking formula, or objective winner is required in v0.4.0; any future aggregation rule must be explicit rather than inferred from the word "pairwise."

If the tested conditions yield nearly identical outcomes and pairwise adds no useful distinction, choose four-way selection to make economical use of one call; do not require a dedicated pairwise primitive in the release merely because it was investigated. Check observed usage and supported charge estimates rather than assuming six calls cost exactly six times as much. If the shapes differ meaningfully for the author's decision, preserve both and teach agents how those differences guide the choice for their human partner. A four-way selection asks for one choice in the presence of all alternatives; pairwise comparison exposes relative preferences under smaller candidate sets. Near-agreement in a bounded spike is not a universal equivalence guarantee. The release decision follows the evidence; retaining both is a hypothesis, not a predetermined outcome. Where both are supported, the agent selects the shape and Sheg validates its distinct packets and reports economics without automatically converting an accepted study.

Economics is part of that investigation. One all-four request may be cheaper than six pair requests, but compare actual usage and supported charge estimates, not image count or call count alone. Pairwise execution repeats narrative/profile input while showing fewer images per call. Its distinct visual contexts cannot be merged by the existing same-state question batching. The agent owns the direct-versus-pairwise study choice; Sheg provides call-fit and selection estimates for the chosen shape. Order-swapped trials, if included, are additional calls beyond the six unordered pairs.

### Additional recipes

The same foundation can test comprehension/application of an explanatory illustration, expectation created by an image before revealing an article, and thumbnail invitation versus interpretability. These are useful guidance examples, not additional release subsystems. Comprehension uses an authored question with a defensible answer or rubric rather than only asking whether something looks clear. Staged expectation questions preserve their exposure boundary. Thumbnail studies use supplied crops/captures and cannot establish actual click-through uplift.

## 4. Material and asset contract

Extend the domain material model with text and static visual items. Existing text objects without a kind discriminator remain valid with their current meaning. New visual items have a stable item ID, an exact asset reference, a required agent/author-supplied factual alt-text descriptor, optional exact caption, and optional presentation provenance. A caption and description are separate fields: the former was shown as authored content; the latter represents image content for a declared textual projection. Neither is a respondent reaction. The operating reasoning agent supplies the descriptor, using an author-supplied descriptor when available; Sheg never generates or infers it. A missing or blank descriptor fails acceptance for new visual material. Its identity and provenance are frozen with the visual.

Assets are supplied through explicit local-file imports with an expected SHA-256 digest, or explicit references to already saved assets in the current Sheg datastore. Inline poll, inline journey, manifest, and follow-on inputs use the same asset-reference contract. Relative manifest paths resolve against the manifest; inline paths are resolved and reported at inspection using the existing entrypoint path conventions. Read local bytes, verify digest and actual supported encoding, and validate dimensions/size before acceptance. A saved-asset reference resolves verified stored bytes rather than an old import path; an unknown reference fails before acceptance. Follow-on selection resolves source assets through its saved context. Remote image URLs are not accepted. Admission and execution do not transcode, resize, crop, download, or substitute an image. Explicit preparation through the scaling tool below produces a separate candidate and digest for the agent to inspect and select.

Support PNG, JPEG, and WebP static images within the verified provider envelope. Reject animated inputs and unsupported formats with actionable errors. MIME claims and file extensions alone do not establish encoding. Preserve exact imported bytes. An asset's byte digest identifies its content; separate material IDs identify authored roles and encounters. Two material items may refer to the same asset with different captions or descriptions. Metadata and encounter placement participate in input identity even when image bytes do not change.

Persist imported bytes in the local SQLite custody boundary as immutable, content-addressed asset records. Accepted run/context records bind to those assets and their frozen metadata. Acceptance is transactional: verify the bytes available to the transaction, save all required assets and context snapshots, then accept the run and lineage together. A changed or missing import fails acceptance without a partially accepted run. This storage choice keeps backup/migration custody under the delivered SQLite contract rather than introducing a separate asset-directory backup protocol.

Accepted workers, resume, evidence inspection, and follow-ons use saved bytes, never reread the original source file. Accepted follow-ons retain all their required asset references independently of source-run deletion. Delete only unreferenced asset records when the owning deletion operation can prove no accepted run/context still needs them. Import paths are provenance, not later dependencies. Inspection returns bounded metadata and a way to retrieve/view a requested saved asset; it does not dump base64 in ordinary status, query, or report output.

Existing source-linked Choice material reuse remains valid for text. Extend it to visual material through explicit option-to-material IDs, without requiring option wording to equal image bytes or a description. Keep the existing exact-text equality rule for text mappings. Visual choices use authored stable option meanings; mapped material snapshots retain the chosen image, caption, description, and provenance. No-fit/unmapped selections retain the existing exclusion semantics.

### Explicit scaling convenience

Provide one shared image-scaling operation through MCP and CLI, backed by a bundled processing dependency that works in the installed plugin without an external image tool or repository checkout. Its purpose is to make a supplied image small enough for supported provider input. It preserves the whole image, aspect ratio, and orientation; it only scales down. No crop, upscaling, content alteration, general editing, format-selection toolkit, or agent-controlled recompression workflow is in scope. Preserve supported input format where the scaling encoder supports it; an unsupported preparation format yields an explicit limitation, not a silent format change. Encoder details and dependency selection belong to planning and must preserve the scaling-only contract.

The agent supplies a local source with expected digest or a saved asset reference and requests smaller dimensions or a target image-byte budget, with optional target-provider context. Use actual encoded output size when checking a byte target. A provider-aware convenience target cannot guarantee complete-call fit: additional images, state, questions, and request framing share the admission budget. Return an explicit inability to meet a target when appropriate, rather than a promise of image fidelity or packet fit.

Preparation preserves the original asset and stores a separately identified candidate with source linkage, source/output digests, dimensions, encoded sizes, and scaling parameters. Return a bounded view/export handle and reusable asset reference so the agent can inspect it and discuss the result with the author. Selecting the candidate for a study is explicit; it receives the supplied descriptor/caption and becomes the exact frozen stimulus. Retention/deletion treats the preparation source as a dependency of retained derived assets so lineage remains inspectable. Do not execute inference, accept a study, or repeatedly alter an accepted asset as a side effect of scaling. Runtime admission checks the complete selected packet. Typography/layout recipes must inspect whether scaling preserved the questioned distinction.

## 5. Exposure, packets, and compact carry-forward

Exposure records text and visuals in encountered order. Default cumulative material retains all previously exposed items, unique by material ID in first-exposure order, without repeatedly delivering every image's pixels. Re-exposure records a new encounter; a task that explicitly presents the visual again delivers saved pixels again. Distinct material items may reference the same bytes with different roles or captions. Binary deduplication may save attachments only when the item-to-ordinal binding preserves each delivered role.

At a visual evaluation, the packet supplies the currently presented image pixels and exact visible caption to a compatible provider. Explicit material/exposure bindings identify which assets that question or independent group evaluates. Newly encountered visual material is available as pixels for that authored visual stage; earlier visual material is compact history unless explicitly selected for pixel reuse. Subsequent sibling questions in one independent group share the same presented image and frozen pre-group history; the first answer does not compact away the pixels needed by siblings.

For subsequent stages, carry a factual record of the visual exposure using its supplied descriptor, exact caption if any, stable material/asset identity, and exact typed responses with authored meanings. For example, the record separately identifies a picture of a yellow flower in an orange pot and the fact that the respondent chose X when it was shown. Preserve chronology and do not invent motives, perceptual details, free-form reactions, or a respondent-authored description. The supplied descriptor is an explicit compression of the visual evidence, not lossless visual memory or a claim about what the respondent noticed.

The generic packet retains respondent profile, ordered material/exposure records, typed trajectory, and the authored question/group. Distinguish encountered assets, pixels delivered now, descriptors delivered as history, and omitted material. Default later questions can use an eligible text provider without receiving historic pixels. If a later question needs visual inspection, the agent explicitly selects saved pixels for that evaluation; Sheg checks capability, attachment count, and fit again. Omitting an item or prior responses remains an independent authored context control. Persisted source exposure evidence remains inspectable even when omitted from a later packet.

The visual attachment map binds each delivered image to its exact asset and ordinal. Pixel-evidence state includes neutral material bindings, exact visible captions, and narrative location. The descriptor for the image under evaluation and private preparation/treatment notes are excluded from that image's pixel-evidence prompt by default; descriptions must not reveal intended answers as hidden visual annotations. Earlier compact history intentionally includes supplied descriptors and discloses that representation. An explicitly authored described-evidence evaluation uses the descriptor instead of pixels and is labelled accordingly. It is a different question condition, not automatic downgrading when pixel admission fails.

Cloudflare places attached images before state. Narrative markers do not claim to render page layout; supplied screenshots carry actual layout evidence. The compiler excludes future/unexposed material, sibling treatments, other respondents, answer keys, and private study labels. Whole-article placement studies deliberately expose the whole article. Independent groups do not append sibling answers. A text-only provider rejects requested pixels before dispatch; it never silently drops them. Cached inputs do not establish persistent image memory.

Inspection exposes the material projection and same compiler/wire bindings used at dispatch. Automatic compact carry-forward is a declared default, frozen as part of the accepted study's context policy. Historical text-only requests retain their existing semantics. Saved pixels remain available for explicit reuse without dependence on the source file.

## 6. Mixed-provider execution, matched branches, and follow-ons

One submitted durable study can contain sequential text/visual stages, independent question groups, and declared matched comparison forks. A stage is an authored exposure/evaluation boundary, not a hard-coded article section or provider route. The agent defines the graph, typed routing rules, branch treatments, shared criteria, comparison membership, material projection, and any provider constraints. Sheg does not infer stages or forks from prose. Existing deterministic response routing remains distinct from a matched fork: a response chooses one route, whereas a matched fork deliberately copies an eligible context into every declared condition.

A matched fork snapshots the actual reached and eligible pre-treatment respondent contexts after its upstream selection is settled. For a finite cohort this means upstream respondents have reached the boundary or ended/failed, rather than accepting a live membership filter that grows as the comparison runs. Each branch copies the same available profile, prior material projection, and typed history for its respondent. It then adds only its own treatment and asks the authored questions. Preserve a common fork/selection identity plus distinct branch/context/evaluation identities. The same respondent ID may participate in several branches; it never implies a single mutable history shared by them.

An article journey can therefore evaluate sections 1 through 3 with eligible text providers, select respondents who reached the end of section 3, and branch their saved contexts into section 4 with versus without an image. The comparison lock selects one admitting provider/model for both arms, including the no-image arm. Earlier providers may differ only outside declared equality constraints. Both conditions ask equivalent authored questions; neither receives the other condition's image or answers. Whole-article hypothetical placement and direct preference remain separate experiment shapes, not substitutes for this staged fork.

Each branch can continue through later stages with its own exposures, compact visual records, and responses. A shared downstream definition can be reused without merging histories: branch identity and context remain distinct. There is no automatic reconciliation of contradictory branch answers into one respondent state. The agent can end branches, author typed continuation rules, or explicitly select branches for additional work. Survivor-only comparisons disclose different denominators. No automatic cohort allocation or branch-winner selection is inferred.

Maintain one study-wide physical-call allowance covering prefix work, every executed branch, batching splits, and retries. Inspection calculates bounded minimum/maximum topology call requirements with matched fan-out included, without enumerating hypothetical response histories. Shared prefix work is executed once, not replayed per branch. Preserve ADR-0032's initial/reached admission split. When a fork's actual cohort is ready, inspect its known treatment packets jointly for the common provider choice. Later sequential packets are checked when reached; initial inspection cannot guarantee their future fit.

Continue unaffected respondents and branches when a condition fails. Preserve successful counterparts and report incomplete matched pairs separately from complete-pair evidence. Recover only failed/pending eligible work under the same saved input and resolved provider, using existing attempt/uncertainty rules and remaining allowance; recovery is not guaranteed if a failure cannot be safely retried. Never replay successful sibling answers, treat transport/fit failure as a respondent choice, silently drop the failed arm from the comparison, or switch providers to rescue it. Cancellation/recovery operate at the study boundary with inspectable member status; exact worker scheduling and API decomposition belong to planning.

Follow-ons remain first-class for later agent-designed extensions. Select exact answered evaluations/context handles, including branch identity, with existing source-version checks; snapshot the selection at acceptance. A follow-on does not retroactively change the source study's treatments, locks, or histories. Independent alternatives branch from the same pre-treatment source rather than chaining variant B from variant A's answer. Retain their material/asset dependencies independently of source-run deletion.

Continue-mode follow-ons retain preceding text and compact visual records by default. Allow an explicit retained-material subset, including empty, independently of prior-answer inclusion. New supplied material provides the next evidence. Reject unknown, unexposed, or sibling material selections; keep selected items in encounter order. Do not reconstruct history omitted by the source packet's policy. Existing recorded, fresh-material, omit-history, selected-Choice material, and text-only continue behavior remain compatible. Recorded mode reproduces the selected source packet's delivery; continuing uses the new compact-history default unless pixels are explicitly selected again.

For an onward text stage or follow-on, the image descriptor, exact caption, and prior typed reaction travel distinctly. Clef evidence remains attributable to Clef, even when Jev or Laya evaluates the next section. Cross-provider confidence scales are not interchangeable, and linked branches do not create independent human observations. Evidence reports original cohort, reached selection, per-condition answers/failures, complete pairs, and onward survivors. No free-form model reasoning, implicit continuation threshold, or sampled routing is introduced.

## 7. Clef adapter, configuration, and credentials

Add an explicit `clef` provider kind to config, factory, evaluation-level execution identity, readiness checks, and worker construction. The proposed route is the documented HTTPS Cloudflare REST account endpoint. Account and model selectors are validated configuration; tokens live in a separate secure credential target through the existing credential boundary. Extend credential status/setup/removal routing as needed without placing secrets in requests, persisted config, command arguments, logs, packets, or reports. Unsupported platforms report secure-store unavailability consistently with current behavior.

Allowlist the Cloudflare origin and derive the account/model path from validated fields. Refuse redirects and arbitrary destinations. A Clef token must not be sent through a Jev route. Freeze allowed configurations and context/selection policy at acceptance; freeze the resolved provider, route, account identity, requested model, admission policy, and exact packet/compiler fingerprint for each execution unit before dispatch. Retain safely observed served-model identity; mismatched served models are an explicit failure. Resume preserves resolved choices, comparison locks, compiler, asset, and projection; unresolved future units use the accepted catalogue and constraints.

Translate Sheg Choice options and Score rubrics into Clef criteria and preserve Noul semantics. Parse the Cloudflare envelope, then validate every returned answer through Sheg's typed contracts. Match exact question and option IDs; validate probability domains/sums, rubric legends, response type, and model identity. Successful siblings survive a malformed sibling according to existing group behavior. Do not relabel Clef execution as Jev or assume its result is a chat completion.

Retain existing maximum physical-call allowance, reservations, observed/uncertain attempt accounting, bounded timeouts, and explicit recovery. One dispatched batch is one physical attempt, regardless of image or question count. Pre-dispatch validation consumes no inference attempt. A timeout after dispatch remains uncertain rather than free. An oversized question group may split under its declared batching control. An oversized state's image count or material does not authorize splitting its evidence into different studies, and exhausted budgets do not authorize a cheaper provider. The planner chooses adapter-local implementation details and error identifiers within these contracts.

## 8. Admission, preview, and evidence

Use the same compiler and wire projection for preview, fit inspection, acceptance, and runtime checks. Check image count, actual encoding, dimensions, per-image/aggregate bytes, encoded request-body bytes, question counts, and typed criteria bounds before dispatch. Deduplicated attachments are counted as sent, with all material bindings still visible. Report violations with item identity and the applicable bound, without dumping image contents.

Text, image tokens, question schemas, and framing share the model input budget. The advertised model window is not a usable hosted admission guarantee. Current documentation permits four embedded images, 4 MiB/16 megapixels each, 8 MiB aggregate decoded image bytes, and 13 MiB request bodies; questions have their own bounds. The hosted schema allows text truncation, and OpenRouter separately warns about an approximately 2,000-token text-state cap. Treat these as route observations to verify, not durable constants implied by model capacity.

A retained hosted integration must establish a conservative effective text-and-media envelope that avoids known truncation. Document how it is derived, whether token counts are measured or estimated, and its limits. Unknown fit is unavailable/unverified, never fits. Initial acceptance and each later reached dispatch require a supported envelope for their actual packets; a successful HTTP response or a plausible answer does not upgrade unavailable admission to verified fit. Local structural inspection remains usable when credentials or provider feasibility are unavailable.

Do not silently trim article text, histories, schemas, or images. If the full article/script does not fit, show what exceeded the admitted envelope and let the agent propose an explicitly smaller study for the author's decision. A bounded excerpt, crop, contact sheet, or reduced question group is a changed input with a new identity. Frozen execution must not apply it automatically on resume.

Evidence inspection identifies source/prepared asset digests and scaling lineage, image ordinals, encountered versus delivered material, captions/descriptors, compact-history versus pixel delivery, prior history policy, comparison locks, branch identity, context-fit basis, and per-evaluation provider/model provenance. Show logical packet evidence separately from the transport projection. Keep request metadata and response diagnostics bounded. Existing query/status/attempt views remain useful; no requirement to expose raw provider responses or duplicate image bytes per evaluation in public output.

## 9. Reporting and interpretation

Compare equivalent authored questions/rubrics over the intended matched respondent basis, retaining stable comparison keys and occurrence handling. Include answered, failed, pending, and unreached coverage, complete/incomplete matched pairs, and per-condition results. Compute paired differences only from complete matching pairs; retain unmatched outcomes visibly without imputing a missing response or labelling an incomplete pair a tie. A caption change, new crop, new provider, or different context projection is visible in the comparison basis. Separate-variant outcomes, direct preference, whole-article hypothetical judgments, and staged transition outcomes are distinct evidence types. No aggregate may silently merge them.

Where comparisons originate from one matched fork or reached-stage follow-on selection, expose that lineage and selected denominator. Within-study branch membership supplies explicit matching; unrelated historic runs do not gain an automatic cross-run matcher. Provider differences and survivor selection remain visible rather than being interpreted as visual-treatment effects.

The useful report connects typed outcomes to the author's choice: where an image supports meaning, where it interrupts continuity, which dimension a sketch communicates, or which presentation is preferred under the authored criteria. No generated free-form Clef rationale is promised. The agent may interpret answer patterns with asset/text inspection, distinguishing observed typed responses from its own explanation.

Describe results as correlated model judgments from frozen profiles and inputs. Probabilities and confidence are model outputs, not population estimates. Expressed interest does not measure actual reading, attention, clicks, comprehension retention, or conversion. Cohorts vary authored perspectives; they do not establish human audience validity. Preserve this language in shipped skills and examples without obstructing ordinary study execution with new ritual approvals.

Do not create a catch-all visual orchestration module or put article/character heuristics in provider code. Follow current layering while allowing focused asset import/storage helpers. Exact field names, helper decomposition, and test file allocation are planning-owned choices; they must implement the described observable contracts rather than defer their meaning.

SQLite schema 9 is the supported v0.3.0 baseline under ADR-0031, superseding ADR-0027's baseline while retaining its forward-migration/recovery policy. Preserve the released migration; append successors through the typed repositories and production migration runner. New asset storage requires sequential tested forward migration, verified SQLite-consistent backup, integrity/foreign-key checks, and transactional failure. Version persisted request/packet payloads independently and upcast only when safe. Historic text-only requests retain their original semantics, packet/compiler identity, answers, and physical-attempt evidence. Never reinterpret an old run as described-visual input. If old execution cannot be resumed safely, refuse it explicitly while preserving reads and recovery.

Keep the existing bounded maintenance surface usable on upgrade failure or unknown future schemas. Extend backup/deletion/custody tests to prove asset preservation. Generated schemas, runtime bundles, packaged helpers, and both shipped skills must travel in the self-contained plugin and operate without a checkout. v0.4.0 version alignment follows the release playbook at release time; this spec changes no manifests.

Consequential accepted choices need individual ADRs and index updates in their owning implementation changes, including multimodal material/custody and scaling provenance, compact visual history, mixed-provider selection and experimental locks, matched-fork execution/recovery, batching controls, and Clef routing/admission. Supersede existing accepted records where contracts change rather than rewriting history. The proposed spec does not prelabel these choices as accepted ADRs before human review.

## 11. Feasibility gates and failure disposition

Direct REST inference with compact images has demonstrated basic image-plus-text transport and position-aware typed character matching. Original-size PNG requests encountered a context-admission rejection; a paid Workers plan did not remove that rejection. These findings motivate, but do not replace, hosted-envelope and perceptual investigation. OpenRouter image delivery remains unproven and outside required routing scope. The roadmap must include bounded, transient investigations with an explicit inference allowance and credentials authorized for that work. This specification supplies questions and required evidence, not an implicit paid-call budget. Keep raw spike outputs outside shipped source; ship meaningful behavioral fixtures, adapter tests, and supported contract notes.

### Hosted contract and admitted input

Verify direct REST authentication/envelope, model identity, Choice/Score/Noul outputs, image binding/order, actual size/count limits, and handling of errors/timeouts. Probe controlled facts near the end of bounded text with varying media and question-schema pressure, including clearly unsupported packets. Combine observed behavior with the actual preprocessing/contract basis; a few correctly answered sentinels alone do not prove lossless admission. Establish the conservative supported envelope and document uncertainty. If no defensible envelope exists, stop retained hosted integration and bring the route/admission decision back for design revision.

### Visual evidence and hypothetical conditions

Use purposeful image/text relationships and contradictory variants. Hold text fixed while changing the visual, then change surrounding text while holding the visual fixed. Check whether placement instructions and image-to-item bindings are respected, including no-fit cases and image omission as a deliberately different condition. Compare singleton questions with independent batches and reordered sibling questions. If batching changes judgments materially for the authored task, design that recipe with singleton execution; automatic fitting splits under the accepted control remain valid, but do not secretly change the recipe's joint-versus-singleton condition or claim sibling independence from schema shape alone.

### Appearance and character distinction

Use obvious rendered differences first, then subtle typeface/size changes, preserving captures before provider processing. Investigate whether resizing erases the questioned difference and whether added plain text lets responses bypass rendered readability. For character studies, use visibly distinct sketches with explicit brief/script evidence and compare unprompted versus prompted contexts. Compare one all-four selection with the six independent pairwise comparisons under the same narrative, respondent, provider/model, and authored criteria. Inspect agreement, disagreement, ties/cycles, coverage, usage and charge estimates without inventing a ranking rule to force agreement. Swap direct-comparison order in explicitly budgeted additional trials. Failures constrain the supported recipe; do not infer typography expertise or narrative fit from plausible preference answers. An unsupported required demonstration needs an explicit scope/design amendment before a v0.4.0 release claim.

### Follow-on composition

Verify exact reached-context selection, independent branches, selected material projection, typed history transfer, and text description/caption/reaction carry-forward. Compare a narrow transition packet with one also containing earlier article prose; report their different evidence basis. Check source deletion, changed import files, worker interruption, and retry without repeating previous sections or rebinding variants. Exercise within-study matched forks as well as later follow-ons, the compact-history default and explicit pixel reuse, frozen-catalogue late selection, shared provider/model locks, study-wide budget accounting, one-arm failure with unaffected progress, and recovery without successful-arm replay. No stage failure may be interpreted as a respondent choosing to stop.

These are feasibility checks on model behavior and system contracts, not validation against human outcomes. The roadmap defines small fixtures, call limits, and observed criteria appropriate to each question. It must preserve failed observations and return consequential changes for human review rather than tune prompts until one attractive answer appears.

## 12. Acceptance criteria

1. An author can inspect and execute a bounded article-plus-image poll with typed questions. Preview and saved evidence bind the actual image, caption, ordered sections, projection, and configured Clef identity. A changed source file after acceptance cannot alter the run. Every visual includes a supplied factual descriptor; Sheg performs no description inference.
2. The five-section article can be assessed at authored hypothetical insertion points over one fully exposed state. Separate and batched execution retain their declared question groups and visibility semantics; no result is called unseen-future continuation.
3. Two supplied rendered variants can be assessed independently over equivalent profiles and questions, and directly compared as a separate task. Saved evidence includes treatment/capture provenance and coverage; sibling variants/answers do not leak across separate contexts.
4. Up to four character sketches can be assessed separately against the same brief or bounded script and directly compared together within the total four-image allowance. The implementation spike also evaluates six independent pairs, exposing only each selected pair and no sibling answers. Near-identical outcomes with no useful pairwise distinction favor four-way selection for economy, without requiring a dedicated pairwise primitive. Meaningful differences justify retaining both and shipping guidance that helps the agent choose for the human's decision. Neither outcome establishes universal equivalence or requires an automatic pairwise ranking. Trait expression, prompted fit, and protagonist recognition remain distinct authored tasks. A five-image direct request fails admission without dispatch or automatic splitting. Larger candidate comparisons are deferred rather than required release acceptance.
5. One study can execute text stages, fork the frozen reached cohort into independent matched Clef image/caption/no-image conditions under one comparison provider/model, and continue each branch through eligible text stages using supplied descriptors, exact captions and typed reactions. Follow-ons support the same evidence boundaries for later experiments. Prefix work is not replayed, branch histories never merge, and lineage, exclusions, projections, survivor populations and per-evaluation provider provenance remain inspectable.
6. Unsupported images, wrong digests, missing descriptions, unsupported provider delivery, exceeded transport/context bounds, and unknown fit fail before dispatch without consuming an inference attempt. Post-dispatch failures preserve charged/uncertain attempts and successful sibling answers.
7. Accepted runs and follow-ons remain readable and usable after original import removal and source-run deletion. Asset deletion respects all remaining references. Backup/migration failure preserves recoverable evidence and the maintenance surface.
8. A released schema-9 datastore migrates forward with its text-only requests, compiler identities, answers, lineage, and attempt accounting intact. Unknown future payloads/schemas remain untouched. Existing text-only behavior stays compatible.
9. CLI and MCP share validation and admission semantics. The installed self-contained plugin includes required runtime, credential support, contracts, visual evidence access, and skills without depending on repository files.
10. Shipped guidance turns the article, rendered A/B, character, and mixed-provider examples into complete small recipes with authored criteria, explicit evidence boundaries, coverage, and appropriate model-result interpretation. Skill-owned behavior tests exercise real workflow choices under repository doctrine.
11. With enabled Laya, Jev and Clef configurations, inspection explains automatic defaults, experimental locks, eligible economical initial selections, and unresolved later choices. Execution can choose local text, larger hosted text, and pixel-dependent providers within one study under the frozen policy. Preferences and explicit overrides take precedence within eligibility; disabled, unknown-fit, unavailable and insufficient-allowance candidates are excluded. No inference is needed to select; no eligible candidate yields actionable setup/fit failure rather than fallback.
12. Selection incorporates complete planned batch execution, not just a cheap unit rate. Automatic fitting splits and explicit singleton execution preserve identical pre-group state and logical evaluation identities. Inspection and actual attempt evidence distinguish planned minimum calls from actual usage; budget exhaustion never silently changes providers.
13. Catalogue/preferences/constraints are frozen and idempotent at acceptance with inspected-fingerprint pinning. Actual choices resolve and persist before dispatch, survive retries and local configuration changes, and bind all declared comparison members to one provider/model by default. Existing explicit provider requests retain their binding behavior. Unknown later fit is not a promise; an incompatible reached packet yields identified zero-call failure without changing a comparison model.
14. The installed MCP and CLI can explicitly scale a supplied image down while preserving its whole composition, aspect ratio and orientation, without an external image utility. The original and separately inspectable candidate retain digests, dimensions and derivation; selecting a candidate is explicit and full-packet admission remains separate. No cropping, hidden recompression workflow, automatic inference, or runtime asset mutation is introduced.
15. A failure in one matched condition preserves its successful counterpart and allows unaffected work to continue. Reports expose incomplete pairs and complete-pair denominators. Recovery targets only eligible incomplete work with its saved provider/packet and remaining shared allowance; successful evaluations and the common prefix are never replayed. Independent branches may continue to later stages with separate histories.

Extend existing responsibility-owned tests where genuine behavior gaps exist. Use mocked wire fixtures for protocol errors and system invariants, deliberate byte/asset fixtures for custody and packet binding, and bounded live probes for perceptual/hosted feasibility. Mocked successful outputs cannot establish visual capability. Run the repository gate before publication. New or extended tests must be shown to fail when the covered behavior is deliberately broken, then restored; tests that only preserve current code shape or cannot distinguish the intended path are invalid. This document ships neither test results nor spike receipts.

## 13. Planning handoff

After human review, use this specification as the design input for an executable v0.4.0 roadmap. Planning determines task boundaries, dependency sequencing, exact request/schema/API names, fixture details, and authorized spike budgets. It must trace domain contracts through import, persistence, compilation, admission, workers, evidence, generated contracts, and installed guidance. Runtime slices include the guidance needed to use them; a final reconciliation checks the combined author journey.

Planning may select implementation details within the stated boundaries. It may not silently change provider route, frozen-policy/reached-selection semantics, comparison locks, explicit scaled-input custody, compact-history defaults, exact admitted-packet requirements, matched-branch isolation/recovery, first-release demonstrations, or mixed-provider composition. Gates with negative findings return for a specific design amendment. No retained implementation starts before this revised architectural direction is approved. Pairwise release support remains a decision based on the bounded investigation, not a preselected outcome.

## Research basis

Provider sources were rechecked on 2026-10-04. They describe mutable upstream contracts, not a tested Sheg integration. Recheck them during the hosted spike.

- [Cloudflare Clef documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/models/clef/) describes the REST route, typed decisions, and advertised model window; the [input schema](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/models/clef/schema-input.json) defines hosted image and question bounds and allows long-state truncation.
- [Released Clef model](https://huggingface.co/Cloudflare/clef) and [encoding implementation](https://huggingface.co/Cloudflare/clef/blob/main/joint_schema_model.py) motivated explicit media-token budgeting, stateless resend, and image/state order distinctions. Local preprocessing is not a hosted-route guarantee.
- [OpenRouter Clef](https://openrouter.ai/cloudflare/clef) warns about hosted text truncation; it does not establish the direct REST envelope. [Cloudflare caching documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/features/prompt-caching/) does not establish a persistent visual-memory contract.
- [Photos as Web Content](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/photos-as-web-content/) and [zigzag layout research](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/zigzag-page-layout/) motivate separating informational imagery, decoration, and scanning. These human studies do not validate Clef.
- [Seductive-details meta-analysis](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-020-09522-4) motivates distinguishing interest from learning; [thumbnail research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.17051) distinguishes invitation and interpretability. These support recipe questions rather than product accuracy claims.
- [Vision-Language Models Can't See the Obvious](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/papers/Huynh_Vision-Language_Models_Cant_See_the_Obvious_ICCV_2025_paper.pdf) motivates testing perceptual distinctions before promising salience/layout judgments; it is not a Clef evaluation.
