# Sheg v0.4.0 visual studies

Status: proposed architectural specification for human review. This consolidates the visual-study discussion and supersedes the [exploratory sketch](2026-10-04-v0.4.0-visual-studies-sketch.md) as the proposed planning input. It does not certify provider feasibility, authorize paid inference, or begin implementation. Implementation follows the stable v0.3.0 release. Approval of this specification supplies the design basis for an executable roadmap; provider feasibility gates remain part of that work.

## 1. Intent and release boundary

Sheg helps an author investigate whether a visual communicates the intended meaning and supports the intended audience's next decision. An article image may earn interest, clarify an argument, or interrupt reading. A rendered page may make a next section easier to identify. A character sketch may express the intended traits or fit the character's actions in a script. These are applications of one domain-neutral foundation: frozen text and visual material, authored typed questions, stable respondent profiles, and inspectable comparisons.

The initial author story is: "My article has five sections and an image between sections 3 and 4. Does it help respondents continue, distract them, or complement the article?" Success means the author can investigate keeping, removing, moving, replacing, captioning, or presenting that image while seeing the evidence, respondent differences, and limits of the comparison. The author owns the decision and the criteria for what the image should accomplish.

The v0.4.0 release includes static image inputs, a Clef decision adapter, capability-driven provider selection, multimodal packet admission and inspection, durable visual custody, visual follow-ons and explicit text carry-forward, comparison evidence, and shipped agent guidance. Article imagery and placement are the first acceptance scenario. Rendered A/B and character brief/script fit are required demonstrations of the same primitives, subject to the perceptual and context feasibility gates below. They do not introduce separate runtimes or hard-coded editorial questions.

The baseline is `origin/develop` at `716e197`, inspected on 2026-10-04. Its package advertises `0.3.0-dev.12`; the user identifies this as the release-candidate baseline. It includes datastore upgrades and the final v0.3.0 guidance. Do not call it a published stable release. Reconcile with the actual released v0.3.0 before implementation. Preserve existing text-only studies, typed answer semantics, independent question groups, graph exposure rules, durable follow-ons, attempt accounting, recovery, and self-contained plugin distribution.

### Non-goals

- Video, animation, browser navigation, automatic screenshot capture, design editing, or image generation.
- A gaze predictor, human reading-time simulator, population estimator, conversion optimizer, or proof of human learning.
- A persistent model respondent, simulated forgetting, or invisible image-memory service.
- Silent provider changes after acceptance, self-hosted Clef deployment, or fine-tuning.
- Free-form reasoning generated by Clef, automatic trait extraction, or an objective character-design winner.
- Comparing five or more character designs through multiple calls or a contact-sheet workaround. Start with up to four designs; investigate larger comparisons in a later iteration.
- A new GUI, spend-accounting subsystem, or changes to the remaining v0.3.0 release work.

## 2. Approach and alternatives

The proposed approach extends material and packet contracts to carry visuals and keeps orchestration in existing polls, journeys, and agent-composed follow-ons. Before accepting each study step, Sheg selects an eligible configured provider under an explicit preference policy or honors an explicit provider override. One accepted run has one frozen provider configuration. An agent can branch a saved reached-stage context into several Clef runs, then continue selected results through Jev or another eligible provider. This serves the discussed value without inventing a new respondent-memory mechanism.

An alternative is a journey engine that selects a different provider at every node within one accepted run. It could make a mixed journey look like one run, but it changes identity, admission, ownership, recovery, and reporting before that convenience is needed. The proposed v0.4.0 selection unit is a submitted poll, journey, or follow-on step; finer steps are composed as follow-ons. A second alternative represents every visual only through a textual description. That helps text carry-forward but cannot test actual layout, typeface, sketch appearance, or image/text contrast. Keep descriptions as explicit projections of preserved assets, not replacements for visual evaluation.

### Provider selection across configured capabilities

The user extends composition to every enabled provider capable of running the selected step. Laya can serve bounded text studies locally without a provider invoice; Jev can serve text studies whose packets exceed the configured Laya envelope; Clef can serve pixel-dependent studies. Future adapters such as Kev should participate through declared capabilities and trade-offs rather than special cases embedded in visual workflows. This does not promise local compute is free or that providers produce interchangeable judgments.

Each configured provider exposes supported input modalities and typed question/group forms, route/model identity, effective admission bounds and measurement availability, setup/readiness state, local or hosted execution, and known cost/operational characteristics with their evidence basis. Model-quality or latency claims require supporting evidence and must not be invented from model name or parameter count. Provider metadata cannot bypass actual packet admission.

Selection first eliminates disabled, unconfigured, unavailable, incompatible, and non-admitting providers, including configurations whose planned minimum physical calls exceed the submitted allowance. Evaluate the complete submitted step across its respondents and question groups, not one convenient packet. A batch-capable configuration can therefore qualify where singleton execution cannot meet the call allowance. Reserve no inference attempts while assessing candidates. It then ranks the eligible configurations under a user-visible policy. An explicit provider override is still checked for compatibility and fit; it never silently falls back. Inspection explains the selected provider and rejected alternatives. If none qualifies, return actionable reasons without dispatching inference. Credential values are excluded from selection evidence.

The agreed ownership boundary is that Sheg manages provider selection on behalf of the agent and exposes controls for the agent to override it. The default posture is economical, with user preferences taking precedence. Eligibility is mandatory: neither a preference nor economy can make a provider able to inspect pixels or fit an oversized packet. Prefer eligible local execution without a provider invoice when no stronger preference applies. Among hosted configurations, use comparable known estimated charges for the complete planned execution, including repeated state across split calls, rather than price per call alone. Estimates have a source, applicability, and freshness basis; they are not billing guarantees or a new spend ledger. Unknown or incomparable prices must be disclosed, and selection then uses a documented deterministic order rather than claiming a cheapest result. Quality and latency remain disclosed characteristics, not invented optimization scores.

Maintain a secret-free local provider catalogue containing explicitly enabled configuration IDs, their connection/model settings, and optional user preference order. Setup of one provider does not enable others or grant access to every published model. Automatic selection considers only enabled entries. Missing catalogue or no eligible entries produces setup guidance; it does not create a hosted default or obtain credentials. Existing explicit provider-config requests remain supported as intentional selections, without requiring users to migrate to catalogue IDs. Any applicable explicit disable policy still requires re-enabling that configuration rather than silently bypassing it.

Agent controls include an explicit provider/configuration, a per-request preferred order among enabled configurations, required modality, and a same-provider/model comparison constraint. An explicit selection is binding. For automatic choice, apply hard constraints first, then per-request preference, then saved user preference, then economy. Inspect the resulting choice before dispatch. Do not turn a preferred-but-ineligible candidate into an override of fit or capability. Freeze the resolved configuration, relevant catalogue/preference snapshot, exclusion reasons, estimate basis, and selection-policy version with the accepted request. Changes between inspection and acceptance are shown through a changed selection fingerprint; the submission can pin the inspected fingerprint and refuse a stale choice. Retries of the same accepted submission return the original resolved selection, even when local preferences subsequently change.

Freeze the selected provider and selection basis at acceptance. Resume preserves them even if enabled providers or preferences later change. Comparisons can pin one provider/model across variants; otherwise disclose differences and do not attribute them solely to the visual treatment. A context that must match another arm's provider is a selection constraint, not a preference to ignore when a cheaper candidate appears. Provider selection itself is non-inference; post-dispatch failures do not trigger automatic cross-provider substitution.

For a submitted multi-arm comparison, automatic choice must admit the required packets for every arm on one provider/model unless the agent explicitly designs a provider comparison. Separately composed follow-on comparisons can inspect alternatives, then pin the resolved provider/model in each submission. Selection of a journey covers its required packet envelope and existing branch/history admission rules; do not select from its small first packet and promise later provider switching when a larger packet fails. Existing unverified traversal limits stay visible and runtime fit checks still apply.

### Question grouping and physical batching

Preserve ADR-0021's division: the agent authors independent questions over the same frozen state, while Sheg plans and executes fitting physical batches for the selected provider. Poll and follow-on groups may split their ordered questions across calls without changing their material or feeding sibling answers into history. Laya's current singleton behavior and Jev's measured batching participate in selection estimates. Journey turns remain sequential. Sheg must not infer independence from similar question wording or combine different respondent contexts.

Add an explicit execution control for automatic batching versus singleton calls within an independent group. Automatic batching is the default and keeps the current fitting-split behavior; singleton forbids joint scoring while preserving logical shared-context/evaluation identities. Provider selection and call-fit inspection honor that control and the original maximum physical-call allowance. Inspection reports the planned minimum calls, included question IDs per planned call, and repeated-input estimate. Actual dispatches remain recorded physical-attempt evidence. Resume may regroup remaining unanswered questions under the accepted batching control and the same provider, as current execution does; it cannot change singleton into joint execution or replay successful sibling answers.

The agent controls semantic dependencies, visibility, grouping, and whether joint evaluation is allowed. Sheg controls wire packing under those constraints. Clef joint-scoring effects belong in feasibility checks and guidance; no claim of statistical independence follows from no sibling-answer history. If a recipe needs singleton evaluation, use the explicit control, not hidden execution changes.

The proposed first Clef transport is Cloudflare Workers AI REST from the existing Node runtime. Use an explicit Clef provider identity, Cloudflare account configuration, and a separately stored credential. Support the documented `clef` and `clef-flash` model selectors without silently swapping them; use `clef` as the initial spike reference model. An enabled `clef-flash` entry may participate in economy-based selection when its capability/admission contract is established. Choosing a selector never enables it implicitly. Clef through OpenRouter and local Clef hosting are outside this release's required routing scope; existing Jev routes remain supported. The REST feasibility gate must establish a usable admitted-input envelope before retained integration depends on it. A route change requires a design amendment rather than an invisible fallback.

## 3. User-value slices and study semantics

### Article imagery and hypothetical placement

An author supplies ordered article sections, the actual image, its caption if present, and clearly labelled insertion points. A shared-state study exposes the whole article and image. Independently authored questions ask how the same respondent would judge placement A, B, and so on. For example: "Assume this image appears only at point A. Would this respondent want to continue from section 1 into section 2?" Exact point definitions and conditional instructions are frozen material/question content. No new placement engine is required.

Separate relevance, continuity, distraction, comprehension, and expressed interest. Include removal or no-fit alternatives when the author's decision warrants them. A Choice can select a placement; Scores can assess authored dimensions; Noul can assess an explicitly defined proposition. No universal attractiveness score or automatic ranking formula is prescribed.

The whole-article study is an informed hypothetical assessment. It cannot be reported as a first encounter with unseen later sections. A staged journey instead exposes only material reached on that path. Independent questions do not append sibling answers to history. Clef's joint scoring still needs validation for sibling-question influence; conceptual independence is not proof of statistical independence.

### Rendered visual A/B

An author supplies frozen captures of two visual treatments, such as different font sizes or image-left versus image-right. Separate-variant evaluation sends A and B in separate contexts with equivalent respondent profiles, prior state, question, rubric, provider, and model. Neither sees the other variant or its answer. Existing matched-arm semantics support the comparison; separately composed runs retain their common source lineage and exact criteria without becoming a new cross-run scoring engine.

A direct comparison shows both variants together and asks an authored preference question. It is a different task. Neutral labels, image order, and any contact-sheet arrangement are recorded inputs. Tie or neither options are available through authored Choice options. Do not equate direct preference with independently judged adequacy.

Capture provenance includes supplied viewport dimensions, capture bounds, render scale, and treatment notes where known. Record unavailable provenance explicitly rather than fabricating it. Font-loading readiness and relevant font identity belong in the author/agent's capture notes. Wrapping, height, or viewport-content changes caused by font size are part of the treatment. Whole redesigns are allowed, but their results concern the bundle of changes. Text supplied beside a screenshot can bypass the screenshot's readability; the agent must select and disclose the appropriate evidence for the question.

### Character brief and script fit

An author supplies a character brief such as "heroic and intelligent" and up to four sketches. Separate authored dimensions reveal whether a sketch communicates one trait while missing another. Additional typed options can investigate unintended impressions. Unprompted trait reading occurs in a context without the target traits; prompted brief-fit occurs in a separate context with them. Neither result substitutes for the other.

A script can supply narrative evidence: behavior, dialogue, relationships, role, and tone. Identify the character being matched when the script contains several. Distinguish matching a specified character from asking which design communicates protagonist prominence. A design can look like a lead while poorly fitting the written character. Ask diagnostic dimensions alongside overall fit where useful; do not promise free-form explanations from the decision model.

Assess up to four sketches separately using the same narrative and respondent basis, or show all four together for an explicitly authored direct comparison. Current hosted Clef permits four images per request; any additional visual context also consumes that allowance. The v0.4.0 recipe stays within this envelope rather than attempting five candidates or using a contact sheet to bypass the scope. Comparing five or more designs across multiple calls is a later iteration that must establish how the comparison remains meaningful across differing visible alternatives. Sheg must not automatically split a direct comparison into unrelated calls. Whole-script feasibility depends on admitted context; any selected excerpt is an explicit change of evidence, not hidden truncation.

Pairwise comparison is a promising authoring primitive within that initial four-design scope. Four designs yield six unordered pairs. Each pair receives the same respondent profile, brief/script evidence, criteria, and pre-comparison history, with only its two candidate sketches exposed. It asks a relative preference question, including ties or neither where appropriate. Pair calls branch independently; earlier pair answers and absent candidates do not become evidence in later pairs. An all-four call instead exposes all four alternatives and asks one selection question. These are distinct experimental conditions, not interchangeable execution plans for the same frozen input. Sheg must not substitute one for the other through automatic batching or economical provider selection.

Spike all-four selection against the complete pairwise set during implementation. The user expects they may differ, but no outcome is established. Keep provider/model and narrative/respondent basis fixed; retain direct choices, pair outcomes, coverage, and presentation-order effects. Pairwise results can contain ties, cycles, or no consistent winner. No tournament, ranking formula, or objective winner is required in v0.4.0; any future aggregation rule must be explicit rather than inferred from the word "pairwise." Use the findings to choose and explain the default study recipe without promising equivalence.

Economics is part of that investigation. One all-four request may be cheaper than six pair requests, but compare actual usage and supported charge estimates, not image count or call count alone. Pairwise execution repeats narrative/profile input while showing fewer images per call. Its distinct visual contexts cannot be merged by the existing same-state question batching. The agent owns the direct-versus-pairwise study choice; Sheg provides call-fit and selection estimates for the chosen shape. Order-swapped trials, if included, are additional calls beyond the six unordered pairs.

### Additional recipes

The same foundation can test comprehension/application of an explanatory illustration, expectation created by an image before revealing an article, and thumbnail invitation versus interpretability. These are useful guidance examples, not additional release subsystems. Comprehension uses an authored question with a defensible answer or rubric rather than only asking whether something looks clear. Staged expectation questions preserve their exposure boundary. Thumbnail studies use supplied crops/captures and cannot establish actual click-through uplift.

## 4. Material and asset contract

Extend the domain material model with text and static visual items. Existing text objects without a kind discriminator remain valid with their current meaning. New visual items have a stable item ID, an exact asset reference, optional authored description, optional exact caption, and optional presentation provenance. A caption and description are separate fields: the former was shown as authored content; the latter represents image content for a declared textual projection. Neither is a respondent reaction. A visual need not have a description until a text-only projection requires one.

Assets are supplied through explicit local-file imports with an expected SHA-256 digest, or explicit references to already saved assets in the current Sheg datastore. Inline poll, inline journey, manifest, and follow-on inputs use the same asset-reference contract. Relative manifest paths resolve against the manifest; inline paths are resolved and reported at inspection using the existing entrypoint path conventions. Read local bytes, verify digest and actual supported encoding, and validate dimensions/size before acceptance. A saved-asset reference resolves verified stored bytes rather than an old import path; an unknown reference fails before acceptance. Follow-on selection resolves source assets through its saved context. Remote image URLs are not accepted. Do not transcode, resize, crop, download, or substitute an image as part of admission. Such changes produce a newly supplied stimulus and digest.

Support PNG, JPEG, and WebP static images within the verified provider envelope. Reject animated inputs and unsupported formats with actionable errors. MIME claims and file extensions alone do not establish encoding. Preserve exact imported bytes. An asset's byte digest identifies its content; separate material IDs identify authored roles and encounters. Two material items may refer to the same asset with different captions or descriptions. Metadata and encounter placement participate in input identity even when image bytes do not change.

Persist imported bytes in the local SQLite custody boundary as immutable, content-addressed asset records. Accepted run/context records bind to those assets and their frozen metadata. Acceptance is transactional: verify the bytes available to the transaction, save all required assets and context snapshots, then accept the run and lineage together. A changed or missing import fails acceptance without a partially accepted run. This storage choice keeps backup/migration custody under the delivered SQLite contract rather than introducing a separate asset-directory backup protocol.

Accepted workers, resume, evidence inspection, and follow-ons use saved bytes, never reread the original source file. Accepted follow-ons retain all their required asset references independently of source-run deletion. Delete only unreferenced asset records when the owning deletion operation can prove no accepted run/context still needs them. Import paths are provenance, not later dependencies. Inspection returns bounded metadata and a way to retrieve/view a requested saved asset; it does not dump base64 in ordinary status, query, or report output.

Existing source-linked Choice material reuse remains valid for text. Extend it to visual material through explicit option-to-material IDs, without requiring option wording to equal image bytes or a description. Keep the existing exact-text equality rule for text mappings. Visual choices use authored stable option meanings; mapped material snapshots retain the chosen image, caption, description, and provenance. No-fit/unmapped selections retain the existing exclusion semantics.

## 5. Exposure, packets, and projection

The journey topology remains expose, ask, and terminal. Exposing a visual records the same ordered encounter evidence as exposing text. Default cumulative material includes all previously exposed items, unique by material ID in first-exposure order. Re-exposure records an encounter event without duplicating the image in the cumulative packet. Same asset bytes used by distinct material items retain each role in the state; the provider packet may deduplicate binary attachment bytes if its ordinal binding map preserves every role.

The generic packet contains respondent profile, ordered encountered material, typed trajectory, and authored question or independent question group. Its visual attachment map binds each material item to the exact attached asset and ordinal. Serialised pixel-evidence state identifies image references, exact visible caption, and narrative location. Descriptions and preparation/capture notes are retained provenance and are excluded from pixel-evidence state by default. If an author wants an annotation to be evidence, the agent supplies it explicitly as exposed text. This prevents an asset description or treatment label from silently revealing the intended answer. Described delivery explicitly includes the description. Cloudflare places attached images before state; these markers describe narrative order and do not claim to reproduce physical page layout. Rendered captures carry actual layout evidence.

The compiler excludes future/unexposed material, other arms, other respondents, answer keys, and private study labels. Whole-article placement studies deliberately expose the whole article. Same-state independent groups contain no sibling-answer history. Include/omit prior responses remains independent of material selection and visual projection. Model packets must be inspectable before dispatch without issuing inference.

Each request declares visual delivery as pixel evidence or described evidence when visual material is present. Pixel evidence supplies the actual saved attachments to a vision-capable provider. Described evidence supplies each encountered visual's explicit description and exact caption as text, retaining its identity and exposure order. A missing description blocks described delivery. Descriptions may be author-supplied or prepared and inspected by the agent; their provenance is frozen and they are never labelled as the respondent's own interpretation. Clef is not required to generate them.

Existing text-only inputs need no new setting. A text-only provider rejects pixel delivery before dispatch; it does not silently drop images or downgrade to descriptions. A described-evidence question is explicitly about that representation. Reports and previews disclose it. A later visual question can use saved pixels again. Pixel resend makes earlier evidence accessible, not newly encountered; it consumes input context again. Provider caches are an optimization only.

## 6. Mixed-provider follow-ons

Use existing evidence selection and durable follow-on snapshots. A Jev journey asks about sections 1 through 3. The agent selects exact answered evaluations/context handles identifying respondents who reached the end of section 3. That selection is captured at acceptance with existing source-version checks. It is not a live filter that silently grows as the source continues.

Each Clef alternative branches from the same pre-image selection with unchanged profile and prior choices. Its new material contains its own image/caption and the next section header. Variant B never branches from variant A's answer. Preserve the common origin and each branch's distinct asset, packet, question, and provider identities. No requirement to replay sections 1 through 3 or introduce provider switching within the original run.

Continue retains preceding material by default. Extend continue-mode follow-on context with an explicit retained-material selection over already encountered source items, independent of typed-answer inclusion. An empty selection intentionally omits all preceding material while retaining the selected source packet's available typed trajectory and appending its selected completed response. New supplied material still provides the transition evidence. This supports the narrower profile-plus-prior-choices transition packet discussed in the riff without pretending omitted prose is remembered. Selected source items remain in encounter order, and source exposure/trajectory evidence remains available in custody even when omitted from the next packet. Never reconstruct prior answers that the selected source packet's history policy omitted. This does not change existing recorded, fresh-material, omit-history, or continue defaults. Reject unknown, unexposed, or sibling material selections. Inspection shows included and omitted source items and identifies the resulting evidence boundary.

A Clef continuation judgment can be an explicit Choice or Score/Noul with an authored exhaustive routing rule. Sheg does not choose a continuation threshold or sample an unrequested route. Failed, pending, and unanswered evaluations are not departures. The agent selects answered continuation results and creates a Jev follow-on with the selected typed reaction appended, section 4 material, and explicitly described visual delivery. Description, caption, prior response meanings, and asset identity travel distinctly. Section 4 answers remain attributable to Jev, with source Clef evidence retained in lineage.

The same respondent ID means a stable authored profile and linked contexts, not one persistent mind. Compact choices are not a lossless article summary. Cross-provider outputs are not corroborating independent human observations and confidence scales are not interchangeable. A survivor-only later comparison must disclose changed survivor populations; report the original cohort, reached selection, answered visual stage, and onward selection separately.

## 7. Clef adapter, configuration, and credentials

Add an explicit `clef` provider kind to config, factory, execution identity, readiness checks, and worker construction. The proposed route is the documented HTTPS Cloudflare REST account endpoint. Account and model selectors are validated configuration; tokens live in a separate secure credential target through the existing credential boundary. Extend credential status/setup/removal routing as needed without placing secrets in requests, persisted config, command arguments, logs, packets, or reports. Unsupported platforms report secure-store unavailability consistently with current behavior.

Allowlist the Cloudflare origin and derive the account/model path from validated fields. Refuse redirects and arbitrary destinations. A Clef token must not be sent through a Jev route. Freeze configured provider, route, account identity, requested model, admission policy, and packet/compiler fingerprint at acceptance. Retain safely observed served-model identity; mismatched served models are an explicit failure. Resume cannot silently change the provider, model, packet compiler, asset, or projection.

Translate Sheg Choice options and Score rubrics into Clef criteria and preserve Noul semantics. Parse the Cloudflare envelope, then validate every returned answer through Sheg's typed contracts. Match exact question and option IDs; validate probability domains/sums, rubric legends, response type, and model identity. Successful siblings survive a malformed sibling according to existing group behavior. Do not relabel Clef execution as Jev or assume its result is a chat completion.

Retain existing maximum physical-call allowance, reservations, observed/uncertain attempt accounting, bounded timeouts, and explicit recovery. One dispatched batch is one physical attempt, regardless of image or question count. Pre-dispatch validation consumes no inference attempt. A timeout after dispatch remains uncertain rather than free. An oversized question group may split under its declared batching control. An oversized state's image count or material does not authorize splitting its evidence into different studies, and exhausted budgets do not authorize a cheaper provider. The planner chooses adapter-local implementation details and error identifiers within these contracts.

## 8. Admission, preview, and evidence

Use the same compiler and wire projection for preview, fit inspection, acceptance, and runtime checks. Check image count, actual encoding, dimensions, per-image/aggregate bytes, encoded request-body bytes, question counts, and typed criteria bounds before dispatch. Deduplicated attachments are counted as sent, with all material bindings still visible. Report violations with item identity and the applicable bound, without dumping image contents.

Text, image tokens, question schemas, and framing share the model input budget. The advertised model window is not a usable hosted admission guarantee. Current documentation permits four embedded images, 4 MiB/16 megapixels each, 8 MiB aggregate decoded image bytes, and 13 MiB request bodies; questions have their own bounds. The hosted schema allows text truncation, and OpenRouter separately warns about an approximately 2,000-token text-state cap. Treat these as route observations to verify, not durable constants implied by model capacity.

A retained hosted integration must establish a conservative effective text-and-media envelope that avoids known truncation. Document how it is derived, whether token counts are measured or estimated, and its limits. Unknown fit is unavailable/unverified, never fits. Acceptance requires a supported envelope for the actual packet; a successful HTTP response or a plausible answer does not upgrade unavailable admission to verified fit. Local structural inspection remains usable when credentials or provider feasibility are unavailable.

Do not silently trim article text, histories, schemas, or images. If the full article/script does not fit, show what exceeded the admitted envelope and let the agent propose an explicitly smaller study for the author's decision. A bounded excerpt, crop, contact sheet, or reduced question group is a changed input with a new identity. Frozen execution must not apply it automatically on resume.

Evidence inspection identifies asset digests, image ordinals, encountered versus delivered material, captions/descriptions, visual delivery mode, prior history policy, context-fit basis, and provider/model provenance. Show logical packet evidence separately from the transport projection. Keep request metadata and response diagnostics bounded. Existing query/status/attempt views remain useful; no requirement to expose raw provider responses or duplicate image bytes per evaluation in public output.

## 9. Reporting and interpretation

Compare equivalent authored questions/rubrics over the intended matched respondent basis, retaining stable comparison keys and occurrence handling. Include answered, failed, pending, and unreached coverage. A caption change, new crop, new provider, or different context projection is visible in the comparison basis. Separate-variant outcomes, direct preference, whole-article hypothetical judgments, and staged transition outcomes are distinct evidence types. No aggregate may silently merge them.

Where compared follow-ons originated from one reached-stage selection, expose that lineage and the selected denominator. The agent can compare their inspectable results; a new automatic cross-run matcher is not required. Provider differences and survivor selection remain visible rather than being interpreted as visual-treatment effects.

The useful report connects typed outcomes to the author's choice: where an image supports meaning, where it interrupts continuity, which dimension a sketch communicates, or which presentation is preferred under the authored criteria. No generated free-form Clef rationale is promised. The agent may interpret answer patterns with asset/text inspection, distinguishing observed typed responses from its own explanation.

Describe results as correlated model judgments from frozen profiles and inputs. Probabilities and confidence are model outputs, not population estimates. Expressed interest does not measure actual reading, attention, clicks, comprehension retention, or conversion. Cohorts vary authored perspectives; they do not establish human audience validity. Preserve this language in shipped skills and examples without obstructing ordinary study execution with new ritual approvals.

## 10. Ownership and compatibility

| Boundary | Responsibility and existing seam |
| --- | --- |
| Domain material and questions | Extend `src/domain/study/stimulus.ts`, study/task validation, and `src/domain/run/request.ts`; retain domain-neutral IDs, typed answers, and explicit mappings. |
| Exposure and compiler | Extend `src/domain/decision/prompt.ts` and journey packet walking; own ordered encounters, attachment bindings, projection, and history separation. |
| Application admission and execution | `src/application/run-inspection.ts`, preview/preflight, run service, and workers assemble the same accepted packet, enforce provider capabilities, and preserve attempt/lifecycle semantics. |
| Provider selection | A focused application selection service consumes enabled configuration, adapter capabilities, packet admission and user/agent preferences; owns selection explanations and immutable resolved configuration, not question authoring. |
| Provider | New focused Clef adapter plus `src/providers/config.ts` and `factory.ts`; own Cloudflare wire format, fit policy, response validation, and transport. |
| Infrastructure | `src/infrastructure/study-loader.ts`, `run-store.ts`, credential helpers, and worker launch support import, transactional custody, versioned persistence, secure credentials, and secret-free local provider configuration. |
| Entrypoints | CLI and MCP expose equivalent input, preview/inspection, asset evidence access, credential readiness, follow-on and error semantics. |
| Consumer contracts and guidance | Canonical `skills/study-design/` and `skills/stimulus-response-polling/` teach author decisions and execute them; generated schemas and plugin outputs derive from source. |

Do not create a catch-all visual orchestration module or put article/character heuristics in provider code. Follow current layering while allowing focused asset import/storage helpers. Exact field names, helper decomposition, and test file allocation are planning-owned choices; they must implement the described observable contracts rather than defer their meaning.

SQLite schema 8 is the supported v0.3.0 baseline under ADR-0027. New asset storage requires sequential tested forward migration, verified SQLite-consistent backup, integrity/foreign-key checks, and transactional failure. Version persisted request/packet payloads independently and upcast only when safe. Historic text-only requests retain their original semantics, packet/compiler identity, answers, and physical-attempt evidence. Never reinterpret an old run as described-visual input. If old execution cannot be resumed safely, refuse it explicitly while preserving reads and recovery.

Keep the existing bounded maintenance surface usable on upgrade failure or unknown future schemas. Extend backup/deletion/custody tests to prove asset preservation. Generated schemas, runtime bundles, packaged helpers, and both shipped skills must travel in the self-contained plugin and operate without a checkout. v0.4.0 version alignment follows the release playbook at release time; this spec changes no manifests.

Consequential accepted choices need individual ADRs and index updates in their owning implementation changes, including multimodal material/custody, visual packet/projection semantics, provider-selection ownership and controls, batching controls, and Clef routing/admission. Supersede existing accepted records where contracts change rather than rewriting history. The proposed spec does not prelabel these choices as accepted ADRs before human review.

## 11. Feasibility gates and failure disposition

Published documentation/code research has run; live Clef inference has not. The roadmap must include bounded, transient investigations with an explicit inference allowance and credentials authorized for that work. This specification supplies questions and required evidence, not an implicit paid-call budget. Keep raw spike outputs outside shipped source; ship meaningful behavioral fixtures, adapter tests, and supported contract notes.

### Hosted contract and admitted input

Verify direct REST authentication/envelope, model identity, Choice/Score/Noul outputs, image binding/order, actual size/count limits, and handling of errors/timeouts. Probe controlled facts near the end of bounded text with varying media and question-schema pressure, including clearly unsupported packets. Combine observed behavior with the actual preprocessing/contract basis; a few correctly answered sentinels alone do not prove lossless admission. Establish the conservative supported envelope and document uncertainty. If no defensible envelope exists, stop retained hosted integration and bring the route/admission decision back for design revision.

### Visual evidence and hypothetical conditions

Use purposeful image/text relationships and contradictory variants. Hold text fixed while changing the visual, then change surrounding text while holding the visual fixed. Check whether placement instructions and image-to-item bindings are respected, including no-fit cases and image omission as a deliberately different condition. Compare singleton questions with independent batches and reordered sibling questions. If batching changes judgments materially for the authored task, design that recipe with singleton execution; automatic fitting splits under the accepted control remain valid, but do not secretly change the recipe's joint-versus-singleton condition or claim sibling independence from schema shape alone.

### Appearance and character distinction

Use obvious rendered differences first, then subtle typeface/size changes, preserving captures before provider processing. Investigate whether resizing erases the questioned difference and whether added plain text lets responses bypass rendered readability. For character studies, use visibly distinct sketches with explicit brief/script evidence and compare unprompted versus prompted contexts. Compare one all-four selection with the six independent pairwise comparisons under the same narrative, respondent, provider/model, and authored criteria. Inspect agreement, disagreement, ties/cycles, coverage, usage and charge estimates without inventing a ranking rule to force agreement. Swap direct-comparison order in explicitly budgeted additional trials. Failures constrain the supported recipe; do not infer typography expertise or narrative fit from plausible preference answers. An unsupported required demonstration needs an explicit scope/design amendment before a v0.4.0 release claim.

### Follow-on composition

Verify exact reached-context selection, independent branches, selected material projection, typed history transfer, and text description/caption/reaction carry-forward. Compare a narrow transition packet with one also containing earlier article prose; report their different evidence basis. Check source deletion, changed import files, worker interruption, and retry without repeating previous sections or rebinding variants. No stage failure may be interpreted as a respondent choosing to stop.

These are feasibility checks on model behavior and system contracts, not validation against human outcomes. The roadmap defines small fixtures, call limits, and observed criteria appropriate to each question. It must preserve failed observations and return consequential changes for human review rather than tune prompts until one attractive answer appears.

## 12. Acceptance criteria

1. An author can inspect and execute a bounded article-plus-image poll with typed questions. Preview and saved evidence bind the actual image, caption, ordered sections, projection, and configured Clef identity. A changed source file after acceptance cannot alter the run.
2. The five-section article can be assessed at authored hypothetical insertion points over one fully exposed state. Separate and batched execution retain their declared question groups and visibility semantics; no result is called unseen-future continuation.
3. Two supplied rendered variants can be assessed independently over equivalent profiles and questions, and directly compared as a separate task. Saved evidence includes treatment/capture provenance and coverage; sibling variants/answers do not leak across separate contexts.
4. Up to four character sketches can be assessed separately against the same brief or bounded script, directly compared together within the total four-image allowance, or compared through six independent pairs. Pairwise packets expose only the selected pair and share no sibling answers; four-way selection remains a distinct condition. The implementation spike compares their outcomes and economics before settling the default recipe, without requiring an automatic pairwise ranking. Trait expression, prompted fit, and protagonist recognition remain distinct authored tasks. A five-image direct request fails admission without dispatch or automatic splitting. Larger candidate comparisons are deferred rather than required release acceptance.
5. A frozen answered reached-stage selection can branch through Clef image/caption alternatives, then selected answered contexts can continue through Jev using explicit descriptions plus exact captions and typed reactions. Common lineage, exclusions, omitted prose, changed survivor populations, and provider provenance remain inspectable.
6. Unsupported images, wrong digests, missing descriptions, unsupported provider delivery, exceeded transport/context bounds, and unknown fit fail before dispatch without consuming an inference attempt. Post-dispatch failures preserve charged/uncertain attempts and successful sibling answers.
7. Accepted runs and follow-ons remain readable and usable after original import removal and source-run deletion. Asset deletion respects all remaining references. Backup/migration failure preserves recoverable evidence and the maintenance surface.
8. A released schema-8 datastore migrates forward with its text-only requests, compiler identities, answers, lineage, and attempt accounting intact. Unknown future payloads/schemas remain untouched. Existing text-only behavior stays compatible.
9. CLI and MCP share validation and admission semantics. The installed self-contained plugin includes required runtime, credential support, contracts, visual evidence access, and skills without depending on repository files.
10. Shipped guidance turns the article, rendered A/B, character, and mixed-provider examples into complete small recipes with authored criteria, explicit evidence boundaries, coverage, and appropriate model-result interpretation. Skill-owned behavior tests exercise real workflow choices under repository doctrine.
11. With enabled Laya, Jev and Clef configurations, inspection explains an eligible economical selection for bounded local text, larger hosted text, and pixel-dependent inputs. Explicit user/agent preferences and overrides take precedence within eligibility; disabled, unknown-fit, unavailable and insufficient-call-allowance candidates are excluded. No inference is needed to select, and no eligible candidate yields setup/fit reasons rather than an automatic fallback.
12. Selection incorporates complete planned batch execution, not just a cheap unit rate. Automatic fitting splits and explicit singleton execution preserve identical pre-group state and logical evaluation identities. Inspection and actual attempt evidence distinguish planned minimum calls from actual usage; budget exhaustion never silently changes providers.
13. Selection is frozen and idempotent at acceptance, respects inspected-fingerprint pinning, and survives preference/catalogue changes on resume. Existing explicit provider requests work unchanged. A constrained matched comparison uses one admitted provider/model across all variants, or fails with the incompatible packet identified.

Extend existing responsibility-owned tests where genuine behavior gaps exist. Use mocked wire fixtures for protocol errors and system invariants, deliberate byte/asset fixtures for custody and packet binding, and bounded live probes for perceptual/hosted feasibility. Mocked successful outputs cannot establish visual capability. Run the repository gate before publication. This document ships neither test results nor spike receipts.

## 13. Planning handoff

After human review, use this specification as the design input for an executable v0.4.0 roadmap. Planning determines task boundaries, dependency sequencing, exact request/schema/API names, fixture details, and authorized spike budgets. It must trace domain contracts through import, persistence, compilation, admission, workers, evidence, generated contracts, and installed guidance. Runtime slices include the guidance needed to use them; a final reconciliation checks the combined author journey.

Planning may select implementation details within the stated boundaries. It may not silently change provider route, provider-selection unit/policy, lossless-admission requirements, source custody, image/text projection semantics, first-release demonstrations, or mixed-provider composition. Gates with negative findings return for a specific design amendment. No retained implementation starts before v0.3.0 is released and this architectural direction is approved.

## Research basis

Provider sources were rechecked on 2026-10-04. They describe mutable upstream contracts, not a tested Sheg integration. Recheck them during the hosted spike.

- [Cloudflare Clef documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/models/clef/) describes the REST route, typed decisions, and advertised model window; the [input schema](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/models/clef/schema-input.json) defines hosted image and question bounds and allows long-state truncation.
- [Released Clef model](https://huggingface.co/Cloudflare/clef) and [encoding implementation](https://huggingface.co/Cloudflare/clef/blob/main/joint_schema_model.py) motivated explicit media-token budgeting, stateless resend, and image/state order distinctions. Local preprocessing is not a hosted-route guarantee.
- [OpenRouter Clef](https://openrouter.ai/cloudflare/clef) warns about hosted text truncation; it does not establish the direct REST envelope. [Cloudflare caching documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/features/prompt-caching/) does not establish a persistent visual-memory contract.
- [Photos as Web Content](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/photos-as-web-content/) and [zigzag layout research](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/zigzag-page-layout/) motivate separating informational imagery, decoration, and scanning. These human studies do not validate Clef.
- [Seductive-details meta-analysis](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-020-09522-4) motivates distinguishing interest from learning; [thumbnail research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.17051) distinguishes invitation and interpretability. These support recipe questions rather than product accuracy claims.
- [Vision-Language Models Can't See the Obvious](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/papers/Huynh_Vision-Language_Models_Cant_See_the_Obvious_ICCV_2025_paper.pdf) motivates testing perceptual distinctions before promising salience/layout judgments; it is not a Clef evaluation.
